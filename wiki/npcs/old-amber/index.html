    Old Amber¶

    "I thank you. You've broken the veil, and I am now free to journey and protect the town that I've been entrusted with."

    Overview¶
    Name Old Amber
    Nature River spirit; the protective spirit of Willowshore
    Form A large, old amber-gold (golden-copper) koi; speaks in an old voice
    Status Free (Session Sixteen) — released from a dream-world imprisonment ward; consumed the Grandmother's seed and lifted the memory curse from all of Willowshore
    First identified Session Fifteen (Donkey, Willowshore Lore 29; Mido)
    First met Session Sixteen, in the Land of Dreams

    Description¶

    Willowshore's "town spirit" is not a spirit of streets and rooftops — it is the river spirit of the waters that surround and feed the town. The rivers made Willowshore: the logging, the fishing, the five-way confluence that mirrors the ley lines below. Old Amber is their guardian, and by extension the town's.

    Those who claimed to have seen it — believe the rumors or don't — described an old amber-golden koi. The fish shape matters: koi fill the ponds of the Yeshou estate, golden fish swim through the town's iconography, and the dream-realm reflection of Willowshore is walked on the surface of water.

    The Old Customs¶

    For generations, Willowshore appeased Old Amber with offerings into the water: rice and other tributes, given on full moons, on festival nights, and at the turning of the seasons. The spirit seemed to answer — the town prospered on its rivers.

    The custom faded as the town declined. A few of the oldest residents still quietly make offerings, but most have stopped — and most, per Mido, have drawn the sadder conclusion:

    "Most of us believe that Old Amber has abandoned us."

    The Silence — and Its Timing¶

    Old Amber went quiet a few seasons — roughly two years — ago. Fishing dropped off. Crops thinned. The rivers stopped feeling watched over.

    The timeline is the load-bearing fact (established by Da Baishan's questioning of Mido, Session Fifteen):

    • The silence began before the oni came to town — before Kurosawa, before Masru
    • It began just before, or around, Cassian Voss's brief visit to Willowshore (~two years ago)

    Whatever silenced the town's spirit predates every named villain of the campaign — putting it on the same suspicious timeline as the second hand: the frantic erasure at the Hollow of Seven Cedars, the moth-wing powder, and the older seal Kurosawa built on top of.

    The silence was a cage. Session Sixteen answered what happened, if not yet who: Old Amber was imprisoned in the Land of Dreams — held beneath the surface of a pool of dream-water inside a ward anchored by five lanterns, each tethered by a gossamer filament to the Mother of a Thousand Wings, who served as the prison's warden. Donkey's Arcana identified the working as "an imprisonment spell cast in the dream world — some type of ritual or pretty powerful magic." Who cast it is unknown. The veil the dream-whisper begged the party to tear was this ward.

    The Freeing (Session Sixteen)¶

    The party never fought the warden head-on. They cut its leash. Littlefinger's rapier took the first and third tethers, Da Baishan's crossbow the second and fourth, and Donkey's natural-20 Ignition burned the fifth. With each cut the water spread, the koi rose closer — a dorsal fin, then a tail — and the moth-warden lost substance, until the last lantern went out and "the dam busts": water poured across the dreamscape and Old Amber swam up out of it to the three dreamers.

    Its words, in an old voice:

    "I thank you. You've broken the veil, and I am now free to journey and protect the city and town that I've been entrusted with for this amount of time."

    Then the warning:

    "There's a curse over the town. There's two. One has been developed and infects the minds of my people. The other is darker — runs beneath this current one. Older, colder. It wasn't part of the first. It moves the way grief moves through a family that stops speaking of it. Quiet in the day. Patient. It has learned to keep a door for itself, and to smile from behind it."

    The party heard smile and thought of Cheng Yesho. Old Amber did not name anyone.

    The Cure¶

    Da Baishan explained the half-plum and asked for the spirit's help. Old Amber: "Can I consume the plum?" Donkey: "Sure, bud." The koi ate it as Willow had — and the dreamscape turned from "Sin City" black-and-white into color and began to dissolve. "I will not forget what you've done for this area."

    In the waking world the effect was immediate and town-wide: the memory curse lifted from every person in Willowshore. The river accelerated, as if dragging the curse downstream; fish leapt into nets; the water brightened; the reeds bloomed. The town now speaks of Old Amber's blessing openly (see Willowshore).

    Open Questions¶

    • Who imprisoned Old Amber, and when? The two-year silence and a dream-world ritual line up with the second hand — the moth-powder erasure at the Hollow — not with Kurosawa's geomancy
    • What is the second curse? "Older, colder"; it "smiles from behind a door." Old Amber knows it exists and knows its nature. It may know more
    • What can a free river spirit do? "I will not forget." The party has not yet asked it for anything else

    Session History¶

    Session Fifteen (2026-09-03)¶

    • Named and described for the first time: Donkey's Willowshore Lore (29) recalled the old koi-spirit and the tribute customs; Mido confirmed the history and the town's belief that the spirit abandoned them
    • The timeline established: silent ~2 years, predating the oni, roughly coinciding with Cassian Voss's visit
    • The route identified: deep slumber via nightshade-and-poppy brew into the Land of Dreams; Donkey and Littlefinger went under from Willow's back room
    • The dreamers called out to the spirit of Willowshore — the echoes answered in the town's voices, and a soft whisper (possibly Old Amber, possibly not) said to tear the veil — moments before the Mother of a Thousand Wings descended over the five tethered lanterns

    Session Sixteen (2026-09-17)¶

    • Seen for the first time: a large golden-copper koi swimming beneath the rippling dream-water inside the ring of five lanterns — Donkey's Arcana (24) identified the lanterns as anchors of an imprisonment ward and the moth-creature as its warden
    • Freed as the party cut all five tethers (Littlefinger ×2, Da Baishan ×2, Donkey's natural-20 Ignition on the last); rose closer to the surface with every cut — fin, then tail — and swam free when the last lantern went out and the warden dissolved
    • Spoke to the dreamers: thanks for breaking the veil, and the warning of two curses — the developed one on the town's minds, and an "older, colder" one that "smiles from behind a door"
    • Ate the last half of the Grandmother's seed at the party's offer and lifted the memory curse from all of Willowshore; the dreamscape turned to color and dissolved. "I will not forget what you've done for this area"
    • The town felt it within minutes: quickened river, leaping fish, blooming reeds — and Willow called a festival that evening to celebrate the spirit's return
