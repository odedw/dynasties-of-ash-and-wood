    Magistrate Atsura¶

    "I am not your enemy. I am not Kurosawa's enemy. I'm here to understand what is going on and what is the truth."

    Overview¶
    Name Atsura (initially reported by Mido's network as "Otsuru")
    Ancestry Tiefling oni (blue-skinned)
    Role Magistrate of the Council of the Magi; agent of the Mirror Tribunal
    Affiliation The Council of the Magi — sent to investigate Kurosawa, not to assist him
    Location Willowshore, operating out of the occupied Yeshou estate
    Status Active ally-in-practice; freed the party from their cage (Session Fifteen); investigation of Kurosawa "nearly complete" — verdict pending the party's choice (Session Sixteen)

    Description¶

    A small blue-skinned tiefling oni woman, perhaps 5'6" — nothing like Kurosawa's seven-foot menace or the Mayor's girth. She wears no war gear: a samurai-style breastplate over light, robe-like clothes that drape through her forearms, maybe a dagger at most. The party's first read from the tree line was "maybe a wizard, maybe an accountant" — and both turned out to be closer than they knew.

    Her most distinctive feature: dense tattoos running up from her wrists in full arm sleeves — and several of them are arcane tattoos, unique among every oni the party has seen. Da Baishan's read (Perception 19): none are offensive. They are abjuration, warding, and scrying work — protective and investigative magic, worn on the skin. One of them visibly shimmered with tracing light when she authenticated the party's documentary evidence.

    What she lacks in stature she holds in rank. Guards hand her their keys when she says "keys." Kurosawa starts shouting when she frees his prisoners — and composes himself the moment she raises a hand.

    Role: The Mirror Tribunal¶

    Atsura carries the full authority of the Council of the Magi and the Mirror Tribunal — the Council's internal-affairs arm, which polices magistrates "who may be using their station or the learned elements from it in a way that is not in accordance to our credo."

    • She was dispatched because of "disturbing echoes and reverberations" — the ripples and spikes of ley-line energy — emanating from the Willowshore area
    • Her assignment is explicitly to find the truth of what Kurosawa is up to; there was already evidence against him before she arrived
    • She has been in Willowshore only two days (as of Session Fifteen) and arrived unannounced — Kurosawa was visibly, briefly shocked to find her waiting outside Dew Drop Petals
    • Her method is evidence-first: "It can't be based on my eyes. Maybe based on your eyes. I need something that is proof."

    Personality¶

    Courteous, dry, and unhurried — she answers Kurosawa's rapid-fire questions with "I'll tell you when I've learned enough." She respects the law even when she plainly dislikes its results: she confirmed the party's Rite of Open Reckoning conviction was "a legal proceeding up to all the codes and conducts therein of that ancient right" (crediting Kurosawa's encyclopedic knowledge of dead statutes with a lawyer's mix of admiration and distaste), then found the seam in it — the sentence confines the party to Willowshore, not to a cage.

    She smiles more than any oni official the party has met. She winked at Boone. The party is still deciding what that means; Boone is not.

    "Unfortunately, no boyfriend. Travels are long, hours are long, and it's a demanding road."

    What She Knows / Has Verified¶

    • The party's evidence is authentic. Her tattoo-magic examined Kurosawa's signed device-commission note and one of the seven cursed copper nails: "There seems to be no arcane or illusionary script... I believe it's authentic. What you said and what this evidence shows seems to align directly"
    • Kurosawa has been planning something for a while — her words — and she believes the energy spike from the area is his influence
    • The party told her about the memory curse on the town (so she knows testimony from townsfolk is compromised) and their plan to lift it
    • She has not been told about the plum, the Grandmother, or the Land of Dreams expedition — only that the party needs "a few hours without Kurosawa snooping around," which she agreed to provide
    • She has seen Masru's trial script — Kurosawa's handwritten instructions for the first Reckoning's verdict — and heard the mayor's public apology (Session Sixteen). Her assessment: the original trial was "a sham, a kangaroo court" that she "cannot defend in any of my verdict"

    The Offer (Session Sixteen)¶

    With the memory curse lifted and the mayor's script in hand, Atsura told the party her investigation was nearly complete — "I have found all the evidence I believe I need to cast my verdict" — and drew the jurisdictional line carefully: "I am not part of the municipality that can free your name. My investigation is on Kurosawa himself and his stance with the Order and Council of the Magi."

    Then she gave them a choice:

    1. She casts her verdict on Kurosawa now. "I don't know exactly what that outcome would be" — though she believes the party has "a good intuition" of it
    2. The party holds its Second Rite of Open Reckoning first, and she casts her verdict afterward

    "The reason I'm giving you this opportunity is I'm not exactly sure you will have an opportunity afterward once my verdict is cast."

    The party asked for time to consult. She agreed and walked off with Masru lobbying at her elbow. Beforehand she made a muttered aside about "5,000 gold pieces" that went unexplained — the price of Masru's commission, a bounty, a fine, or something else entirely.

    The party is split on her. Boone: "I think she's great." Donkey: "She hands us our freedom on a silver platter, and there's this other curse Kurosawa was investigating... she's up to something. We need to know more about this old curse before we do any deals with her."

    Relationships¶

    Boone¶

    Whatever "psychic connection" Boone attempted through the doorway (Performance — she smirked and shook her head), the private garden conversation worked: she called his eyes honest, took his evidence seriously, and freed the party on his word plus paper. His parting smolder earned a smile and a wink. Boone walked away "on clouds." Willow has already offered him the full head-to-toe treatment: "You will look irresistible."

    Kurosawa¶

    Colleagues in title, adversaries in fact. He did not know she was in town; his beat of visible surprise at the door — caught by Boone — was the first crack in his composure the party has ever seen. She outranks him in practice: she countermanded his caging of the party to his face, and he swallowed it.

    The Party¶

    "I am not your enemy." She is using them as an evidence source against Kurosawa, and they are using her as legal cover — both sides know it, and so far the arrangement is holding. She freed them from the cage on her own ruling: "They are confined to the borders of Willowshore. That is what they were convicted of. And they will honor that."

    Open Questions¶

    • What verdict is she building toward — and what does a Mirror Tribunal sentence against a magistrate of Kurosawa's stature look like?
    • How much of the truth can the party afford to give her? The plum, the spirit realm, and their own rule-bending (the estate heist, the stolen ritual pages) are all still off the books
    • The name discrepancy — Mido's network reported her as Otsuru; she introduces herself as Atsura. Bad intel, or two names?
    • Is Boone right? (Increasingly, embarrassingly: yes)

    Session History¶

    Session Twelve (2026-07-16)¶

    • First observed from the tree line northwest of Willowshore during the party's pre-heist surveillance
    • Noted commanding guards (including Captain Akoto) from the Yeshou estate, unarmed, in Kurosawa's absence
    • Not encountered directly; the party's estate heist that night avoided all contact

    Session Thirteen (2026-08-13)¶

    • Named by Mido's intelligence network (as "Otsuru"): a young tiefling oni magistrate of the Council of the Magi, who arrived leading a new group
    • The party matched her to the administrator they had watched from the trees: "Yeah, we noticed her"
    • Reported to be studying in the estate, questioning Masru, and commanding the town's other oni leaders — all of whom defer to her despite her youth

    Session Fifteen (2026-09-03)¶

    • Formally introduced herself — as Magistrate Atsura — waiting with the guard detail outside Dew Drop Petals when Kurosawa marched the party out to their new cage. His flash of surprise on seeing her (caught by Boone, Perception 22) confirmed she was not part of his plan
    • Identified by Boone's lore (19) as an agent of the Mirror Tribunal — the Council of the Magi's internal affairs — meaning Kurosawa knows exactly why she's here
    • Came back to the courtyard cage alone within the hour to interview the party: "I'm giving you an opportunity to tell me what is your truth" — while confirming she'd verify everything against Willow's testimony, the townsfolk, and her own arcane instruments
    • Took Boone to a private garden table, where he presented Kurosawa's signed device-commission note and a cursed copper nail; her arcane tattoo traced the parchment and pronounced it authentic. Her verdict: "You have honest eyes. I believe you're telling the truth here"
    • Freed the party from the cage on her own legal reading: the Open Reckoning sentence confines them to Willowshore's borders, not to a cell — "Whether you're in a cage or not... we are obeying the intent of that law." Demanded the guard's keys twice; got them twice
    • Faced down Kurosawa publicly when he emerged shouting at the sight of the party walking free — one raised hand, a quiet exchange, and he went back inside
    • Agreed to keep Kurosawa occupied for several hours while the party worked on lifting the curse: "He and I have a few things to discuss anyway"
    • Answered Boone's "Is your boyfriend also a magistrate?" with a chuckle and a workaholic's lament; answered his parting smolder (Performance 13) with a smile and a wink

    Session Sixteen (2026-09-17)¶

    • Walked out of the estate three abreast with Masru and Kurosawa as the lifted curse rippled through town; was hugged by Willow — surprised, didn't push her off, smirked
    • Shut Kurosawa down publicly a second time. When he demanded of Masru why convicted "fugitives" were free to roam, she answered for the mayor: "They haven't broken any of the rulings cast down on them from the Rite of Open Reckoning. They haven't left the city limits and they will not. They are abiding by the ruling"
    • Witnessed Masru's confession and the handover of Kurosawa's verdict script to Da Baishan; the mayor visibly played to her while surrendering it
    • Declared her investigation "nearly complete" and laid out the offer: her verdict now, or the party's Second Reckoning first — warning that the window may close once she rules. Granted the party time to consult: "Very well"
    • Left with Masru pleading for his commission to be voided so he can return to Kirahata with Meilin; her answer, if any, was not heard
