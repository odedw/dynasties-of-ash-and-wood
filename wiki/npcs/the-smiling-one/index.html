    The Smiling One — Cheng Yesho¶

    "Treats."

    Overview¶
    Name Cheng Yesho (the GM has also rendered it Chang Yeshao)
    Title The Smiling One
    Ancestry Human
    Affiliation Willowshore Ruling Family
    Role Family member; Mido's son (see note below); decorated military veteran
    Location Silver Mist Lodge, Willowshore
    Status Under the party's suspicion as of Session Sixteen — Old Amber described a second curse that "smiles from behind a door"

    Brother or son?

    Session Five established Cheng as Mido's brother. In Session Sixteen the GM corrected the party mid-scene — "It's her son" — and Mido spoke of him as a boy she sent to war and got back changed. This page now follows the Session Sixteen ruling; earlier session entries below retain the original wording.

    Description¶

    Cheng Yesho—known around Willowshore as "The Smiling One"—is Mido's son and a member of the Yeshou family. He is perpetually smiling, no matter the circumstances. When misfortune befalls the oni or their allies, his expression brightens. When the new regime stumbles, he seems quietly pleased. He is always in the background, always watching, always grinning.

    Up close (Session Sixteen): average height — about 5'10" — with a childlike face under the enormous smile, eyes that look "blank a little bit," head tilted, a slight bobbing motion, and no evident reading of the room. He speaks in single words. He walks behind people rather than beside them, matching their gait step for step, and smiles whenever they look back.

    He is a decorated military veteran who received a gold medal with gem inlays for bravery beyond the call of duty during his younger years. The medal was signed by a general and describes a distinguished act of valor. Mido's account of what the war did to him: "He was a good, honorable boy, fighting a war he probably shouldn't have been at, and he came back from it like this. Harmless. A happy — might I say, dull — young man."

    The Smile Behind the Door¶

    When the freed river spirit Old Amber warned the party of a second, older curse on Willowshore, it did so in language the whole table heard as a description of Cheng:

    "Darker — runs beneath this current one. Older, colder. It wasn't part of the first. It moves the way grief moves through a family that stops speaking of it. Quiet in the day. Patient. It has learned to keep a door for itself, and to smile from behind it."

    Combined with Boone's Session Six vision — Cheng at the Battle of Five Crows, chanting at the demon while everyone else fled or fought, the smile forming on his face as he stared straight at Boone — the party's working theories at session's end were:

    • Cheng is the vessel of the second curse: whatever he chanted to on that battlefield came home with him, and has kept "a door for itself" behind his face ever since
    • Cheng, or the Yeshou family, is deliberately "trying to bring back that horrible Elder God" — and the family story about a harmless, war-damaged boy is cover
    • Cheng is simply "affected by it," not the cause — Braedon's position walking in, and the one he abandoned within twenty minutes of watching him questioned

    Direct questioning produced nothing from Cheng — and visible nervousness from Mido.

    Knowledge¶

    Cheng has experience with the Chthonic language (the Devil's tongue) and was able to translate the term Gosembiki (五芒星, the Five Pointed Star) for his sister—the name Magistrate Kurosawa gave to haunted ruins east of Willowshore.

    He also claims to know the true ending of the oldest story about the forest shadows—a tale whose ending is never spoken aloud because it embarrasses the ruling family.

    Relationships¶

    Mido¶

    His mother and the family matriarch (see the note above on the brother/son correction). Cheng has served as a translator for Chthonic matters. She protects him — calls him harmless — and was the only person in the room who looked nervous when he was questioned about the moth-wing powder.

    The Party¶

    When Littlefinger returned his military medal, Cheng came forward—still smiling—and took it back with obvious delight. He has never spoken to the party at length; in Session Sixteen his entire vocabulary was "Okay" and "Treats." Da Baishan found him unsettling to walk with; Littlefinger has decided to interrogate him.

    Session History¶

    Session Zero (2026-01-16)¶

    • Established through world-building as "someone who always smiles when the news is bad"
    • Connected to the ruling family and the secret ending of the forest story

    Session Four (2026-02-26)¶

    • Present among the Yeshou family at the Festival of Returning Spirits transfer ceremony

    Session Five (2026-03-12)¶

    • Identified as Cheng Yesho, Mido's brother and a military veteran
    • His military medal for bravery was found in a display case at the Yeshou estate (now occupied by Kurosawa) and stolen by Littlefinger
    • Littlefinger returned the medal to him; Cheng took it with a smile and obvious delight
    • Translated the Chthonic term Gosembiki (Five Pointed Star) for Mido—the name the magistrate gave to ruins east of Willowshore
    • Confirmed to have experience with the Chthonic language

    Session Six (2026-03-19)¶

    • Boone had a vivid dream of the Battle of Five Crows—a massacre from the old war—in which a younger Cheng was performing a chanting ritual over a circle inscribed in mud. With each repetition, his face twisted further into an unnatural grin that would never leave him. The ritual ripped open the sky and summoned a demonic entity
    • When asked about the Battle of Five Crows, his eyes dilated briefly—recognition, fear, or excitement—but his permanent smile never changed; gave a shallow nod of acknowledgment
    • When told the prophecy ("the artist, the ruler, the sage, the lover, the judge"), reached for paper and wrote: "Are you sure you heard it correctly?"—implying the exact wording matters
    • Ginkgo caught Cheng glancing at Mido before answering (Perception 20)—the same kind of loaded look exchanged between Mido and Kurosawa at the festival
    • Wrote two words: "The old ones"—referring to the five ancient gods banished by the current pantheon
    • Looked directly at Ginkgo when the cleric said "the old gods" aloud
    • His permanent smile is now understood to be the physical consequence of the battlefield ritual—his face distorted during the chanting and never recovered

    Session Sixteen (2026-09-17)¶

    • Named by implication. The freed Old Amber described a second, older curse on Willowshore that "has learned to keep a door for itself, and to smile from behind it" — the party immediately thought of him. Now the prime lead on the second hand behind the moth-powder erasure at the Hollow of Seven Cedars
    • Located by Da Baishan's Eyes of the City + Leveraging Connections: alone at the edge of a crowd hanging festival decorations on the north side of town, smiling at nothing. Agreed to come to the inn for "treats and food and ale" (Request 11): "Okay"
    • Walked behind Da Baishan the whole way, copying his gait step for step and smiling whenever the orc looked back. "This guy's starting to creep me out a little bit"
    • Sat beside Mido at the Silver Mist Inn: "Treats." — "Not yet. Those are for the festival." Clapped
    • Reclassified as Mido's son by the GM ("It's her son"); Mido: he "wasn't always like this" — a good, honorable boy who came back from a war he shouldn't have fought "like this... harmless... a happy, might I say, dull young man"
    • Interrogated by Littlefinger: told that Da Baishan recarved Cassian Voss's name at the Seven Cedars and that Voss's ghost said the oni didn't kill him — no reaction (Littlefinger high Perception; Donkey 27). His mouth opened: "Treats." Offered a treat in exchange for explaining the mustard moth-wing powder packed into the erased name — "Kurosawa didn't do that. Who did?" — still nothing
    • Mido, not Cheng, was the one who got nervous. Session ended there
    • Post-session, Boone re-confirmed the Battle of Five Crows vision with the GM: while others fled or fought the demon, Cheng stood chanting at it, the smile spreading Grinch-wide across his face, staring at Boone. The table's closing theory: "Is this family the ones trying to bring back that horrible Elder God?"
