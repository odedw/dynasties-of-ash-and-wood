## Description

A tall figure in tattered robes of old mustard yellow, with dark black eyes. It floats rather than walks. When it attacks it doesn't strike like a boxer or claw like a beast. It **reaches into its target** as if to take hold of the heart or the soul, with no visible force or muscle behind it. When it was hurt, its tatters came apart like parade confetti and spattered **ectoplasm** across the walls. Blades met *"a slight resistance"* passing through it, and nothing like flesh or bone.

The party had seen one before. Its tatters matched what they saw from across the river in Session Zero, the thing that **killed [Cassian Voss](cassian-voss.md)**.

---

## Lore (Da Baishan's Devise a Stratagem, Session Eighteen)

- A ghostly assassin with **a single mission**: take out whoever it's been assigned to
- Legends differ on why. It may be bound in a kind of purgatory until the job is done, or it may be acting under a curse
- **Weak to vitality and holy damage** (most of all), and to **fire**
- **Resistant to everything else**, which is why the party's weapons did little

---

## The Fight (Session Eighteen)

It came for Boone in his room before dawn, after his dream of the [chair by the lake](../locations/carcosa.md). His plum wine jug lay empty on its side. Its first reach was a **critical for 18**. Boone woke the inn shouting, and the party fought it in the hallway.

- [Ginkgo's](../pcs/ginkgo.md) **Fear** failed against it (critical success on its save). His **Vitality Lash** dealt **18** and tore it open
- When it turned on Ginkgo, Boone's **reactive strike** cut it for 16. It grabbed Ginkgo's skull with both hands, but he tore free (Fortitude 25)
- [Littlefinger's](../pcs/littlefinger.md) poisoned sling stones and Boone's polearm chipped at it while it was flanked
- [Da Baishan](../pcs/da-baishan.md) finished it by **throwing a healing potion** (Athletics 23, critical; 8 of 8). It burned, thinned, and smouldered away to nothing

Littlefinger, afterward: *"They didn't send him to kill us. They sent him to kill the Judge."*
