## Description

A **small blue-skinned tiefling oni woman**, perhaps 5'6" — nothing like [Kurosawa's](magistrate-kurosawa.md) seven-foot menace or the [Mayor's](mayor-masru.md) girth. She wears no war gear: a samurai-style breastplate over light, robe-like clothes that drape through her forearms, maybe a dagger at most. The party's first read from the tree line was *"maybe a wizard, maybe an accountant"* — and both turned out to be closer than they knew.

Her most distinctive feature: **dense tattoos** running up from her wrists in full arm sleeves — and several of them are **arcane tattoos**, unique among every oni the party has seen. [Da Baishan's](../pcs/da-baishan.md) read (Perception 19): none are offensive. They are **abjuration, warding, and scrying** work — protective and *investigative* magic, worn on the skin. One of them visibly **shimmered with tracing light** when she authenticated the party's documentary evidence.

What she lacks in stature she holds in **rank**. Guards hand her their keys when she says *"keys."* [Kurosawa](magistrate-kurosawa.md) starts shouting when she frees his prisoners — and composes himself the moment she raises a hand.

---

## Role: The Mirror Tribunal

Atsura carries the **full authority of the Council of the Magi and the [Mirror Tribunal](../factions/council-of-the-magi.md#the-mirror-tribunal)** — the Council's internal-affairs arm, which polices magistrates *"who may be using their station or the learned elements from it in a way that is not in accordance to our credo."*

- She was dispatched because of **"disturbing echoes and reverberations"** — the ripples and spikes of ley-line energy — emanating from the Willowshore area
- Her assignment is explicitly **to find the truth of what Kurosawa is up to**; there was already evidence against him before she arrived
- She has been in Willowshore only **two days** (as of Session Fifteen) and arrived unannounced — Kurosawa was visibly, briefly *shocked* to find her waiting outside Dew Drop Petals
- Her method is evidence-first: *"It can't be based on my eyes. Maybe based on your eyes. I need something that is proof."*

---

## Personality

Courteous, dry, and unhurried — she answers Kurosawa's rapid-fire questions with *"I'll tell you when I've learned enough."* She respects the law even when she plainly dislikes its results: she confirmed the party's Rite of Open Reckoning conviction was *"a legal proceeding up to all the codes and conducts therein of that ancient right"* (crediting Kurosawa's encyclopedic knowledge of dead statutes with a lawyer's mix of admiration and distaste), then found the seam in it — the sentence confines the party to Willowshore, not to a cage.

She smiles more than any oni official the party has met. She winked at [Boone](../pcs/boone.md). The party is still deciding what that means; Boone is not.

> "Unfortunately, no boyfriend. Travels are long, hours are long, and it's a demanding road."

---

## What She Knows / Has Verified

- **The party's evidence is authentic.** Her tattoo-magic examined Kurosawa's signed device-commission note and one of the seven cursed copper nails: *"There seems to be no arcane or illusionary script... I believe it's authentic. What you said and what this evidence shows seems to align directly"*
- **Kurosawa has been planning something for a while** — her words — and she believes the energy spike from the area is *his* influence
- The party told her about the **memory curse** on the town (so she knows testimony from townsfolk is compromised) and their plan to lift it
- She has *not* been told about the plum, the [Grandmother](the-grandmother.md), or the Land of Dreams expedition — only that the party needs *"a few hours without Kurosawa snooping around,"* which she agreed to provide

## Relationships

### [Boone](../pcs/boone.md)
Whatever "psychic connection" Boone attempted through the doorway (Performance — she smirked and shook her head), the private garden conversation worked: she called his eyes **honest**, took his evidence seriously, and freed the party on his word plus paper. His parting smolder earned a smile **and a wink**. Boone walked away *"on clouds."* [Willow](radiant-willow.md) has already offered him the full head-to-toe treatment: *"You will look irresistible."*

### [Kurosawa](magistrate-kurosawa.md)
Colleagues in title, adversaries in fact. He did not know she was in town; his beat of visible surprise at the door — caught by Boone — was the first crack in his composure the party has ever seen. She outranks him in practice: she countermanded his caging of the party to his face, and he swallowed it.

### The Party
*"I am not your enemy."* She is using them as an evidence source against Kurosawa, and they are using her as legal cover — both sides know it, and so far the arrangement is holding. She freed them from the cage on her own ruling: *"They are confined to the borders of Willowshore. That is what they were convicted of. And they will honor that."*

---

## Open Questions

- **What verdict is she building toward** — and what does a Mirror Tribunal sentence against a magistrate of Kurosawa's stature look like?
- **How much of the truth can the party afford to give her?** The plum, the spirit realm, and their own rule-bending (the estate heist, the stolen ritual pages) are all still off the books
- **The name discrepancy** — Mido's network reported her as *Otsuru*; she introduces herself as *Atsura*. Bad intel, or two names?
- **Is Boone right?** (Increasingly, embarrassingly: yes)

- **Faced down Kurosawa publicly** when he emerged shouting at the sight of the party walking free — one raised hand, a quiet exchange, and he went back inside
- Agreed to **keep Kurosawa occupied for several hours** while the party worked on lifting the curse: *"He and I have a few things to discuss anyway"*
- Answered Boone's *"Is your boyfriend also a magistrate?"* with a chuckle and a workaholic's lament; answered his parting smolder (Performance 13) with a smile and **a wink**
