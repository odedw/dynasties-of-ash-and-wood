## Description

Willowshore's "town spirit" is not a spirit of streets and rooftops — it is the **river spirit** of the waters that surround and feed the town. The rivers made Willowshore: the logging, the fishing, the five-way confluence that mirrors the ley lines below. Old Amber is their guardian, and by extension the town's.

Those who claimed to have seen it — believe the rumors or don't — described an **old amber-golden koi**. The fish shape matters: koi fill the ponds of the Yeshou estate, golden fish swim through the town's iconography, and the dream-realm reflection of Willowshore is walked *on the surface of water*.

---

## The Old Customs

For generations, Willowshore **appeased Old Amber with offerings into the water**: rice and other tributes, given on **full moons**, on **festival nights**, and at the **turning of the seasons**. The spirit seemed to answer — the town prospered on its rivers.

The custom faded as the town declined. A few of the oldest residents still quietly make offerings, but most have stopped — and most, per [Mido](mido.md), have drawn the sadder conclusion:

> *"Most of us believe that Old Amber has abandoned us."*

---

## The Silence — and Its Timing

Old Amber went quiet **a few seasons — roughly two years — ago**. Fishing dropped off. Crops thinned. The rivers stopped feeling *watched over*.

The timeline is the load-bearing fact (established by Da Baishan's questioning of Mido, Session Fifteen):

- The silence began **before the oni came to town** — before [Kurosawa](magistrate-kurosawa.md), before [Masru](mayor-masru.md)
- It began **just before, or around, [Cassian Voss's](cassian-voss.md) brief visit** to Willowshore (~two years ago)

Whatever silenced the town's spirit **predates every named villain of the campaign** — putting it on the same suspicious timeline as the *second hand*: the frantic erasure at the [Hollow of Seven Cedars](../locations/hollow-of-seven-cedars.md), the moth-wing powder, and the older seal Kurosawa built on top of.

**The silence was a cage.** Session Sixteen answered *what* happened, if not yet *who*: Old Amber was **imprisoned in the [Land of Dreams](../locations/land-of-dreams.md)** — held beneath the surface of a pool of dream-water inside a ward anchored by **five lanterns**, each tethered by a gossamer filament to the **Mother of a Thousand Wings**, who served as the prison's **warden**. Donkey's Arcana identified the working as *"an imprisonment spell cast in the dream world — some type of ritual or pretty powerful magic."* Who cast it is unknown. The **veil** the dream-whisper begged the party to tear was this ward.

---

## The Freeing (Session Sixteen)

The party never fought the warden head-on. They cut its leash. Littlefinger's rapier took the first and third tethers, Da Baishan's crossbow the second and fourth, and Donkey's natural-20 **Ignition** burned the fifth. With each cut the water spread, the koi rose closer — a dorsal fin, then a tail — and the moth-warden lost substance, until the last lantern went out and *"the dam busts"*: water poured across the dreamscape and Old Amber swam up out of it to the three dreamers.

Its words, in an old voice:

> *"I thank you. You've broken the veil, and I am now free to journey and protect the city and town that I've been entrusted with for this amount of time."*

Then the warning:

> *"There's a curse over the town. There's two. One has been developed and infects the minds of my people. The other is darker — runs beneath this current one. Older, colder. It wasn't part of the first. It moves the way grief moves through a family that stops speaking of it. Quiet in the day. Patient. It has learned to keep a door for itself, and to smile from behind it."*

The party heard *smile* and thought of [Cheng Yesho](the-smiling-one.md). Old Amber did not name anyone.

### The Cure

Da Baishan explained the half-plum and asked for the spirit's help. Old Amber: *"Can I consume the plum?"* Donkey: *"Sure, bud."* The koi ate it as [Willow](radiant-willow.md) had — and the dreamscape turned from *"Sin City"* black-and-white into color and began to dissolve. *"I will not forget what you've done for this area."*

In the waking world the effect was immediate and town-wide: the **memory curse lifted from every person in Willowshore**. The **river accelerated**, as if dragging the curse downstream; fish leapt into nets; the water brightened; the reeds bloomed. The town now speaks of Old Amber's blessing openly (see [Willowshore](../locations/willowshore.md#the-spirit-of-willowshore)).

## Open Questions

- **Who imprisoned Old Amber, and when?** The two-year silence and a dream-world ritual line up with the *second hand* — the moth-powder erasure at the Hollow — not with Kurosawa's geomancy
- **What is the second curse?** *"Older, colder"*; it *"smiles from behind a door."* Old Amber knows it exists and knows its nature. It may know more
- **What can a free river spirit do?** *"I will not forget."* The party has not yet asked it for anything else
