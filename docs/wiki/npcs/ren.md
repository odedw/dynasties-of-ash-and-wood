## Description

An oni soldier of the town garrison, city-born and city-raised, who considers Willowshore *"the most backward town I've ever been."* He approached the party during the festival from behind [Da Baishan](../pcs/da-baishan.md), speaking so that it wouldn't look like the two were talking.

---

## Testimony

Ren stood watch on the **east river gate** (the road along the river toward the [Gosembiki ruins](../locations/gosembiki-ruins.md)) on **the night before the cataclysm**:

> *"The magistrate walked through that gate. I reported it — but the log says he never entered that gate."*

His report is the "one irregularity" [Captain Akoto](captain-akoto.md) admitted under [Kurosawa's](magistrate-kurosawa.md) own questioning at the Second Rite of Open Reckoning. Akoto filed the mismatch between Ren's report and the magistrate's estate log with the **[Mirror Tribunal](../factions/council-of-the-magi.md#the-mirror-tribunal)**, and that filing is why [Atsura](atsura.md) was sent to Willowshore.

---

## Goals

- **Get out of Willowshore.** He doesn't want the reward money Da Baishan's Sow Rumor promised. He wants *"an assignment out of here."*

---

## Relationships

### The Party
A walk-up witness. [Ginkgo](../pcs/ginkgo.md) asked whether he'd testify: *"If that gets me an assignment out of here, I'm willing to testify."* The party has implicitly promised to help him get a transfer.

### [Captain Akoto](captain-akoto.md)
His commanding officer, who believed his report, filed it by the book, and later confirmed it in open court.

## Session History

### Session Seventeen (2026-10-08)
- **Came forward at the festival** in answer to Da Baishan's Sow Rumor, but refused the reward: he had stood on the east river gate the night before the cataclysm, seen the magistrate pass through, reported it, and later found the log said Kurosawa never entered
- Agreed to testify **in exchange for a reassignment** away from Willowshore
- His report, filed upward by Captain Akoto to the Mirror Tribunal, turned out to be the reason Atsura came to town. It was the decisive fact at the Second Rite of Open Reckoning

### Session Eighteen (2026-10-15)
- Helped carry the drunken [Masru](mayor-masru.md) home to the estate after the festival
- [Atsura](atsura.md) credited the Sow Rumor with *"uncovering Ren"* and his testimony with helping the case, while warning Da Baishan off false rewards
