## Description

Luda is an older woman who runs a modest shop in [Willowshore](../locations/willowshore.md). Her official trade is seamstress work—clothing repair, fabric, patches, general goods—but she also deals in rare plants, mushrooms, and alchemical ingredients. She has enough knowledge of alchemy and arcane components to supply spell-casters with the specialized inks, powders, and botanical materials needed for scrolls, spell transcription, and other magical crafting.

She is not a wizard or a practitioner, but she knows what the practitioners need and where to find it.

---

## Personality

Luda is blunt, suspicious of authority, and unapologetically hostile toward the oni presence in Willowshore. She does not mince words or soften her opinions. She views the entire oni regime as corrupt and self-serving.

---

## Relationships

### Donkey
Luda is one of Donkey's regular suppliers for spell components—special inks, crushed fungi, and other arcane crafting materials. They have a transactional but trusting relationship. When Donkey asked her to report on [Kurosawa's](magistrate-kurosawa.md) future purchases, she agreed without hesitation.

### [Magistrate Kurosawa](magistrate-kurosawa.md)
Kurosawa demanded arcane ingredients from Luda in high volume with extreme specificity. He paid her, but the interaction was cold and intimidating. She is deeply concerned about the purpose of the ingredients and considers the oni's presence in Willowshore a facade for something sinister.

## Session History

### Session Five (2026-03-12)
- Introduced as Donkey's regular supplier for arcane crafting components
- Revealed that [Magistrate Kurosawa](magistrate-kurosawa.md) demanded **high-volume, highly specific arcane ingredients** from her—she was compensated but disturbed by the cold interaction and the nature of the request
- Believes the oni presence is a facade for a nefarious arcane purpose
- Holds an extremely low opinion of all the oni, especially Kurosawa
- Agreed to track and report any future purchases by the magistrate to Donkey

### Session Eight (2026-04-22)
- Called as a **prosecution witness** during the Rite of Open Reckoning—visibly reluctant to testify at all
- Admitted under [Kurosawa's](magistrate-kurosawa.md) questioning that she had **never trusted him personally**
- Kurosawa then turned the questioning: Luda confirmed that **Donkey had asked her to spy on the magistrate** and report his purchases; she said she *"didn't feel she had a choice"*
- Refused to condemn the party outright: *"I don't think they can be seen as deceiving."* — not a defense, but not the condemnation Kurosawa was hoping for either
- Her testimony was the most awkward for both sides: it damaged both the magistrate's impartiality and the party's deniability about surveilling him

### Session Fifteen (2026-09-03)
- Met [Donkey](../pcs/donkey.md) at her door with a scowl and a ledger: *"I think you still owe me seven gold pieces"* (the true figure may have been four; she stood at seven; he paid). *"Excellent. The counts are settled. How can I help you today?"*
- **Supplied the [Land of Dreams](../locations/land-of-dreams.md) expedition**: three doses of **nightshade and pressed poppy petals** for 9 gold — cut with the proper instruments, prepared in her back room in fifteen minutes, potency preserved. She knew exactly what the brew was for and asked no questions beyond *"how many doses?"*
- Handled the transaction with professional discretion: *"Please don't loiter around and look obvious"*
- Sent Donkey off with the campaign's most on-brand herbalist blessing: *"Thank you, my old friend — and I hope you have powerful dreams"*
- Was **pickpocketed for 6 of the 9 gold** by [Littlefinger](../pcs/littlefinger.md) on the way out (a 29; she didn't notice). The party's net cost for the entire expedition: three gold
