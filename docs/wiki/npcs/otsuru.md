## Description

A **small blue-skinned oni woman**, perhaps 5'6" — nothing like [Kurosawa's](magistrate-kurosawa.md) seven-foot menace or the [Mayor's](mayor-masru.md) girth. She carries **no visible weapons**: no nodachi, no katana like the red-skinned guardsmen wear. The party's first read from the tree line was *"maybe a wizard, maybe an accountant"* — bookkeeping, paperwork, quiet authority. [Mido's](mido.md) contacts describe her as a **young tiefling oni woman** who arrived leading a new group of her own.

What she lacks in stature she holds in **rank**. She works out of the estate, questions [Masru](mayor-masru.md) and others, and **issues orders that are obeyed** — even [Captain Akoto](captain-akoto.md) and the town's other oni leaders follow them. *"She is awfully young,"* Mido noted, *"and surprisingly they all defer to her."*

---

## What Is Known (via Mido, Session Thirteen)

- Her name is **Otsuru**, and she is a **magistrate** — like Kurosawa — of the **[Council of the Magi](../factions/council-of-the-magi.md)**
- She arrived **leading a new group** of oni; the garrison roughly doubled around the time she appeared
- [Mido](mido.md) initially feared she was the **exile escort** Kurosawa promised for the Yeshou family *"ages ago"* — she no longer believes this
- She has been **"studying things" in the estate** and talking to [Masru](mayor-masru.md) and other town figures
- She is *"either looking for or affiliated with [Kurosawa](magistrate-kurosawa.md) in some way"* — Mido cannot yet tell which
- Mido's verdict: *"Yet another either possible adversary or ally amongst our midst"*
