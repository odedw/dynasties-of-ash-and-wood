# Okio

*"Darling, it's unlocked."*

---

## Description

At first glance: a woman of about forty, long jet-black hair, silk kimono with etched designs — aristocratic dress, not farm clothes. She stoops over a halfling stove because the cabin was built for Littlefinger. She cooks well; the stew smelled like a welcome.

Under the robe: additional legs. In magical darkness she bites with a jaw that opens too wide, injects poison that leaves pus at the wound, and fights with slashing limbs. She can snuff a room into a sphere of magical dark that smothers lantern-flame and daylight.

---

## Personality

Polite trespasser first: apologies, coins for supplies, a husband *"out foraging,"* a story about fleeing spiders in the hills on the way to Willowshore's **centennial**. When pressed, she drops the mask fast — foreign phrases, darkness, then open hunger. She taunts those who cannot see. She claims the cabin for her brood: *"Our children will be stronger."*

---

## Goals

- Nest in the cabin with her web-lurker mate
- Raise stronger offspring (her parting threat)
- Possibly drawn by the same **centennial / ley-line** spectacle she described — whether that cover story was partly true is unknown

---

## Relationships

### The Web Lurker ("Husband")
The bipedal spider-keeper who ran the hunter-spider ambush on the road to the [Hollow of Seven Cedars](../locations/hollow-of-seven-cedars.md) and fled when the party turned the fight. He hid in the cabin rafters under tarp and netting until she called him down. Donkey immobilized him with a critical **Tangle Vine** while Boone and Donkey escaped.

### The Party
Boone and Donkey (and a hiding [Meilin](meilin.md)) interrupted the nest. Boone cut off one of her legs and fled. She kept the cabin. Littlefinger wants it burned.

### [Masru](mayor-masru.md)
Unwitting possible victim: Da Baishan sowed a rumor that [Meilin](meilin.md) was alone at the cabin during the Yeshou feast. Masru left dinner early on a *"very strong lead."* Whether he reached Okio is unresolved.
