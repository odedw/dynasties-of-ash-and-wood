## Reaching It

Tian Xia's spirit realm is active and near — and per [Mido](../npcs/mido.md), the veil between worlds is thinnest **in the deepest sleep**. Ordinary sleep is not enough: the traveler must be sunk so deep that shaking cannot wake them.

The known method (recalled by the party via [Luda's](../npcs/luda.md) herb-craft and Boone's field medicine):

- A brew of **nightshade** (*"which brings about the dreams"*) and **blessing of the poppy** (which drops the drinker into near-unwakeable slumber)
- The ingredients must be **cut with the right instruments** and pressed carefully to keep the nightshade's potency; careless preparation *"can cause death"*
- One dose yields an estimated **two to four hours** under, depending on potency and the sleeper's size
- Crafted as a Medicine/Nature working — the party's batch (Boone 30, Ginkgo 31 with Guidance) produced three syringe-small vials of dark bluish-black liquid

**Communication across the veil works — barely.** [Ginkgo's](../pcs/ginkgo.md) Message cantrip reached the sleeping Donkey as a faint, far-away voice (from inside the dream: *"like the voice of God, with a little giggle"*). Physical sensation does not cross: hand-squeezing, ear-poking, and nose-picking were all rigorously tested and all failed.

### What Crosses Back (Session Sixteen)

- **Wounds are real, in both directions.** A claw in the dream opened a visible **gash on Littlefinger's sleeping body**; a failed Will save against a fear-vision **stopped Donkey's heart for a second or two** on the massage bed. Physical damage taken in the dream is physical damage
- **Healing from outside works — for the body only.** Boone's Battle Medicine on the sleeping Littlefinger (22, with Robust Health) restored the slashing damage he'd taken, and he felt it inside the dream. Mental damage could *not* be treated from outside
- **Mental damage carries over** into waking — but once mind and body are reunited it becomes healable like any other
- **Conditions do not carry over**: Donkey's frightened state ended on waking
- A sleeper wakes when the dream **dissipates** (as it did when Old Amber was freed); whether one can wake at will was never tested

## The Dream of Willowshore

What the first expedition found (Session Fifteen):

- **Footing like glass over water** — each step ripples outward across an invisible surface
- Below (and mirrored **above**, folded over like the city in *Inception*): [Willowshore](willowshore.md) as a **dark, flat, warped painting**, roughly 30 feet overhead
- No sun, no compass directions, no reliable gravity — an ambient **purplish glow** lights everything from nowhere
- **Words echo back in the town's voices.** Donkey's call — *"Spirit of Willowshore, we seek your aid"* — returned from every direction in his own voice, Willow's, Kurosawa's, Luda's, Mido's

### The Five Lanterns and the Veil

Answering the second call (Diplomacy 22), five **orange lanterns** kindled in the distance, floating as if on water, each trailing a **filament tether up into the darkness**. A soft whisper — *not* one of the echo-voices — delivered the realm's only instruction so far:

> *"You must tear the veil. The veil must be broken."*

Donkey's Arcana (22) placed the reference: **the veil** appears in Willow's ceremonial dance at the Festival of Returning Spirits — the play of the founders' fragile bargain — performed the night a **moth swarm destroyed five front lanterns** at the theater. The dream restages the town's founding pageant, tethers and all.

### What Holds the Tethers

When Donkey approached a lantern (it drifted away from his reach), he looked up — into **two fly-like eyes** and a wingspan unfolding down out of the darkness: a moth-creature **made of thousands of moths**, tethered to all five lanterns. The party knows its names: the **Mother of a Thousand Wings**, *the Unspeakable One* — the pestilence demon whose moth-wing traces have marked every corner of the mystery since the [Mourndusk Willow's](dark-woods.md) ritual bowl.

Session Fifteen ended with the dreamers face to face with her.

### The Prison of Five Lanterns (Session Sixteen)

Donkey's Arcana (24) read the structure the moment the session resumed:

- **Inside the ring of lanterns, the glass floor was water** — rippling, with real depth, *"like looking down at a pond or an aquarium"* — and beneath it swam a **large golden-copper koi**: [Old Amber](../npcs/old-amber.md), imprisoned
- Only Donkey could see the **arcane arcs and lines** drawn between the five lanterns: an **imprisonment spell cast in the dream world** — *"some type of ritual or pretty powerful magic"* — each lantern an anchor of the prison and of its containing ward. Who cast it is unknown
- The Mother of a Thousand Wings was **the warden** — *"a guard or a ward of this area... a defender of this space"* — leashed to the five lanterns by the same gossamer filaments she guarded. She could not stray far from them
- **Every tether cut weakened both bindings equally** (Da Baishan's Perception, via Skill Stratagem): the veil on Old Amber loosened, and so did the warden's leash — she moved more freely with each lantern that went out, and lost substance with each one too, *"ghostly holes"* opening in the swarm

**The warden's arsenal**, as experienced: claws (12 and 9 slashing on Littlefinger); a **Draconic-language spell** that shows the target a vision of a companion's death (Will save; 12 mental damage and frightened 1 on Donkey — who saw Littlefinger's wound yellow, turn to moths, and be absorbed); and a **shriek** (Will save; 11 mental damage and stupefied 2 on Da Baishan and Littlefinger). She critically saved against Sleep (35) and critically failed against **Dizzying Colors** — stunned, blinded, dazzled. Nobody ever dealt her meaningful damage; one crossbow bolt for 8 was the sum of it.

**The veil torn.** Five tethers, five cuts: Littlefinger's rapier (first and third), Da Baishan's crossbow (second and fourth), Donkey's natural-20 Ignition (fifth). Each severed filament faded to nothing, its lantern went out, the water spread further across the glass — a river's sound rising from nowhere, a dorsal fin breaching, then a tail. When the last lantern died the warden **turned black and faded into nothing**, *"the dam busts,"* and water poured across the dreamscape. Old Amber swam free, spoke, and — offered the [Grandmother's seed](../npcs/the-grandmother.md#the-seed-the-plum) — ate it. The dream went from *"Sin City"* black-and-white to color and dissolved, lifting the dreamers back into their bodies.

## Open Questions

- **Who built the prison?** A dream-world imprisonment ritual, roughly two years old, guarded by the campaign's demon — on the *second hand's* timeline, not Kurosawa's
- **Was the whisper Old Amber?** It asked for exactly what freed it; the spirit never confirmed
- **Is the Mother of a Thousand Wings gone** — or only the warden-aspect that was bound to the lanterns? *"He will dissipate one way or another"* is all the GM said
- Can a sleeper wake at will? Still untested — the party has always been woken *by* the dream ending
