## Voss's Account (Session Seventeen)

From [the field journal](../npcs/cassian-voss.md#the-field-journal-session-seventeen) found under [Cheng's](../npcs/the-smiling-one.md) floorboards:

> *"Most convergences are wells. A few are shorelines. A shoreline borders a lake that lies beneath every lake, and across that lake stands a city, under two suns and black stars. When the five veins open together, a living man could walk there from this very square. No death needed. Only the hour, and an invitation."*

He wrote its name, **Carcosa**, then struck it out: *"Better not to write it. Names are how it finds you."* Cheng's additions: ***THE LAKE IS UNDER THE RIVER*** and ***NOT A CITY / A MOUTH***.

[Da Baishan's](../pcs/da-baishan.md) Occultism (19): a name rarely spoken. People call it a dream world or an alternate plane, *"a destination, or an origin, of some type of cult"* tied to an ancient religion.

## The Dream (Session Eighteen)

The night after the Second Rite of Open Reckoning, the people marked by the mustard moth dreamed the same place:

- **The lake.** Flat water that goes on forever and doesn't move when the wind blows. It's *"like solid"*
- **Two suns**, low on the horizon and a little askew: **one red, one mustard**
- **Black stars.** The sky is dark and the stars darker. Instead of giving light, they seem to absorb it
- **The city.** Towers across the water, unlike any city the dreamers have seen. Not elaborate or opulent: *"stark, strong, dire"*
- **The chairs.** At the water's edge, a seat for each of the marked. Boone's is **a judge's chair carved from pale wood**, with an embroidered **mustard-yellow cushion**, *"exactly your size."* Hong says *"a chair is for him"*

Boone heard his own verdict echo across the water, loud and then fading: *"...bother the town no more."* The dream was vivid enough that he wasn't sure he was asleep. He woke to [the soul eater](../npcs/soul-eater.md) standing over him.

**Willow painted it in her sleep**: the suns and the city of towers, in brushstrokes she doesn't recognize as her own. *"It's like it's someone else's."*

## Open Questions

- **What are the chairs for?** *"Five must fall before the world is born anew."* Is sitting the same as falling?
- **The hour and the invitation.** The marks may be the invitation. What is the hour? All five veins opening together?
- **"A mouth."** Cheng's correction. What does it eat?
- **What does the Land of Dreams have to do with it?** The party has already reached one dream realm through [deep sleep](land-of-dreams.md). This one seems to reach out to dreamers by itself

!!! note "Out-of-game aside"
    In Robert W. Chambers' *The King in Yellow*, Carcosa is a city of twin suns and black stars on the shore of Lake Hali, ruled by the King in Yellow (in Pathfinder, linked to **Hastur**). The chapter that brought the dream was titled *Marks for the King*.
